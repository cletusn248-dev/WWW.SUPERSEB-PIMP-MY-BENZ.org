Built Around The Benz.

SUPERSEB — PIMP MY BENZ is centered on one clear automotive direction: Mercedes-Benz.

The brand brings together customization, repairs, upgrades, diagnostics, vehicle sourcing, vehicle sales, inspection, parts and related customer services under one focused experience.

The goal is straightforward — give Mercedes-Benz owners and buyers a place where they can discover services, request help, explore vehicles, plan customization and manage their automotive journey in a more organized way.

Our Story

Why SUPERSEB Exists.

SUPERSEB is designed to bring several Mercedes-Benz needs together instead of treating customization, maintenance, vehicles and support as completely separate experiences.

01 — PASSION

Mercedes-Benz First

SUPERSEB is not positioned as a general automobile platform. The focus is Mercedes-Benz and the people who own, drive, buy and love these vehicles.

02 — EXPERIENCE

From Pimping To Care

A Mercedes-Benz can need much more than styling. SUPERSEB brings customization, repairs, upgrades, diagnostics, inspection and vehicle services into one ecosystem.

03 — DIRECTION

One Connected Platform

The long-term direction is to make it easier for customers to request services, discover vehicles, keep records and interact with the business digitally.

Direction

What We Are Building.

SUPERSEB combines automotive expertise with a digital-first customer experience.

Our Mission

To provide a focused Mercedes-Benz experience covering customization, service, vehicle support and customer tools while keeping the process clear and professional.

Our Vision

To build a trusted Mercedes-Benz ecosystem where owners and buyers can discover vehicles, personalize their Benz, request services, access records and stay connected with SUPERSEB.

Brand Standards

What SUPERSEB Stands For.

These principles shape the way the SUPERSEB brand presents its services and customer experience.

01

Quality Workmanship

The work should be approached with care, attention and respect for the vehicle.

02

Genuine & Premium Parts

Where applicable, customers can be guided toward genuine or premium quality parts and components.

03

Fast & Reliable Service

04

Clear Scope

Customers should have a clear understanding of the service scope before applicable work proceeds.
