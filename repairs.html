Repair Workflow

From Request To Completion.

A repair request should move through clear stages so you can understand what is happening.

01 — SUBMITTED

Request

You submit the vehicle problem and relevant information.

02 — CONTACTED

Review

The request can be reviewed and additional information requested.

03 — INSPECTION

Assess

Where required, the vehicle can move through inspection or diagnostics.

04 — QUOTATION

Quote

Applicable work and quotation details can be prepared before approval.

05 — COMPLETED

Finish

Completed work can be recorded as part of the vehicle service history.
